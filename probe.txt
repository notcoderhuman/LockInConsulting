[39] Conditional English language proficiency admission
[40] If you are applying to a graduate program, have supplied an ELP test but you are slightly below the required score, conditional admission may be offered.
[41] If you are applying with a sponsorship that includes funding for English language studies, conditional admission may be offered.
[42] ELP exemptions
[43] You are exempt from submitting an English language proficiency examination score if any one of the following conditions is true at the time of application to Waterloo:
[44] You have completed three or more years of post-secondary education at a Canadian institution or university,  either at the time of application or by the start of your program at the University of Wate
[45] You have completed a graduate degree at a Canadian university or will have completed it by the start of your program at the University of Waterloo .  or
[46] You have completed three or more years of post-secondary education, or completed a graduate degree in an exempt country listed below by the start of your program at the University of Waterloo .  or
[47] You have been employed for at least three years in a position in which English was the language of business in a country listed below.  or
