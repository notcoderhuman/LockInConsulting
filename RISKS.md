*Note on `SAR` and `AED` pegs:* Both currencies maintain official fixed USD pegs (`1 USD = 3.75 SAR` via Saudi Central Bank; `1 USD = 3.6725 AED` via Central Bank of the UAE), which is why several European central banks omit them from daily floating reference tables.
