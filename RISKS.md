## Exchange-rate source review (15 Oct 2026)

Our 11 destinations use 9 foreign currencies (`USD`, `GBP`, `CAD`, `EUR`, `AUD`, `NZD`, `SAR`, `AED`, `PLN`), converted to `INR`:

| Candidate (`fx_providers.slug`) | Official URL & Terms URL | Terms & Redistribution Summary | Currency Coverage (vs 9 required + INR) | Update Cadence | Unresolved Gaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **European Central Bank (`ecb`)** | [ECB Reference Rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html) · [ECB Disclaimer & Copyright](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html) | Public ECB statistics may be reused free of charge provided the source is quoted (`"Source: European Central Bank"`) and statistics are not distorted. Published for information purposes only; transaction use is discouraged. | Pivots on `EUR`. Covers **`USD`, `GBP`, `CAD`, `EUR`, `AUD`, `NZD`, `PLN`, `INR`** (7 of 9 destination currencies via EUR cross-rate). | Daily around 16:00 CET on TARGET business days. | **Does NOT publish `SAR` (Saudi riyal) or `AED` (UAE dirham).** |
| **Frankfurter (`frankfurter`)** | [frankfurter.dev](https://frankfurter.dev/) · [License & Terms](https://frankfurter.dev/license/) | Open-source MIT software. Explicitly states: *"Frankfurter doesn't own the rates, so it can't give you rights the provider doesn't. If a provider publishes terms of use, they apply. ... Blended rates are Frankfurter's own calculation, not an official rate from any institution."* | Depends on upstream provider (`/v2/providers/ecb`, `/v2/providers/imf`, etc.). | Daily (follows upstream central bank). | Cannot be approved as a standalone legal licensor; must be scoped to an approved upstream source (such as ECB or IMF). |
| **Financial Benchmarks India (`fbil`)** | [FBIL Reference Rates](https://www.fbil.org.in/#/refrates) · [FBIL Terms](https://www.fbil.org.in/#/termsandcondition) | Publishes RBI reference rates in `INR`. Website terms restrict commercial redistribution, framing, and systematic extraction without prior written permission/licensing from FBIL. | Pivots on `INR`. Covers **`USD`, `GBP`, `EUR`, `AED`** (plus `JPY`, `RUB`, `IDR`). | Indian business days (delayed on public portal). | **Missing `CAD`, `AUD`, `NZD`, `PLN`, and `SAR`**, and commercial reuse requires FBIL permission. |
| **International Monetary Fund (`imf`)** | [IMF Exchange Rates](https://www.imf.org/external/np/fin/ert/GUI/Pages/CountryDataBase.aspx) · [IMF Copyright and Usage](https://www.imf.org/en/about/copyright-and-terms) | Under *Special Terms for Statistical Data*, users may download, extract, copy, create derivative works, publish and distribute IMF Exchange Rate Data provided it is attributed (`"Source: International Monetary Fund"`) and any material transformation (such as cross-rate calculation to INR) is explicitly stated. | Pivots on `USD` / `XDR`. Covers **all 9 destination currencies (`USD`, `GBP`, `CAD`, `EUR`, `AUD`, `NZD`, `SAR`, `AED`, `PLN`) + `INR`**. | Daily on reporting business days. | Representative rates are reported by participating central banks; holidays in individual countries mean some currencies may lag by 1–3 business days (still within the 7-day freshness window). |

*Note on `SAR` and `AED` pegs:* Both currencies maintain official fixed USD pegs (`1 USD = 3.75 SAR` via Saudi Central Bank; `1 USD = 3.6725 AED` via Central Bank of the UAE), which is why several European central banks omit them from daily floating reference tables.
